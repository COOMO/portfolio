Problem問題

"I would rather keep writing by hand than use something I can't read."「我寧可繼續手寫，也不想用看不懂的東西。」

The owner, to every POS salesperson店主對每一個 POS 業務說的話
  • Facebook and Instagram posts went viral. Orders arrive by phone, Messenger and walk-in. Messages piled into the thousands.FB 與 IG 貼文爆紅。訂單從電話、Messenger、現場進來。訊息累積上千則。
  • Everything went into one handwritten notebook. Finding an order meant putting on reading glasses while a queue waited.全寫進一本手寫簿。找一筆單要先戴上老花眼鏡，後面還排著隊。
  • Off-the-shelf POS systems failed on small text and nested menus, and none let her correct an order the way she does on paper, by crossing it out.市售 POS 字太小、選單一層又一層，也沒有一套能像她在紙上那樣用塗改修正。

Solution解法

  • Search by name or the first digits of a phone. Surnames sorted by how often they appear in the shop.用姓名或電話前幾碼找單。姓氏依店內出現頻率排序。
  • Orders, kiln batches and walk-in stock in one SQLite file. Over-booking triggers a warning and nothing more.訂單、窯次、現場庫存在同一個 SQLite。超量只會警告，不會擋單。
  • The pickup calendar colours each day by bread count. The kiln tab shows demand per flavor per bake day.取件日月曆依麵包數上色。窯次頁顯示每個出爐日各口味的需求。
  • Every change can be undone, which is the notebook's crossing-out carried over.每個變更都能還原，等於把訂單簿上的塗改搬進來。

Key screens主要畫面

Pick flavors選口味

  • Eight cells, 80 × 80 px, price and ± on each. Total updates live.八格，80 × 80 px，每格有單價和加減。合計即時更新。
  • A dotted cell adds a one-off flavor without touching the catalogue.虛線格加臨時口味，不動主目錄。
  • Three steps: customer, flavors, details. One button per page.三步：客戶、口味、細節。每頁一顆主按鈕。

Pickup-day calendar取件日月曆

  • Each day shows its bread count. Four bands: ≤ 80, ≤ 130, ≤ 160, more.每格顯示當天麵包數。四級：≤ 80、≤ 130、≤ 160、更多。
  • Non-bake days marked and dimmed. Today tagged 今.非出爐日標記並淡化。今天標「今」。
  • Answers "can we take Saturday?" with the phone still in hand.電話還在手上，就能回答「禮拜六接得了嗎」。

Details and confirm細節與確認

  • ±day and ±week buttons move the date without opening the calendar.±日、±週 按鈕直接調日期，不用開月曆。
  • Source chips: LINE, FB, 鄰居, 路過. Pickup time optional.來源：LINE、FB、鄰居、路過。取件時間可不填。
  • Phone optional. Regulars are saved by name only. The owner asked for this.電話可不填。熟客只記姓名。店主要求的。

Manage管理

  • Multi-select pickup dates. Filter by status. Sort by last edit or pickup time.多選取件日。依狀態篩選。依最後修改或取貨時間排序。
  • Virtualised list: months of orders scroll without lag on the tablet.虛擬化列表：幾個月的訂單在平板上滾動不卡。
  • Payment status toggles on the card. Partial payment with amount.付款狀態在卡片上切換。可部分付款並記金額。

Kiln plan窯次

  • Dial a customer from the tablet through a paired phone over Bluetooth.從平板透過配對的手機用藍牙撥給客人。

  • Layer rule: ui → services → db. Services never import ui.分層規則：ui → services → db。services 不碰 ui。
  • The UI thread never awaits the database. A slow query or an upload cannot freeze the counter.UI 執行緒不 await 資料庫。慢查詢或上傳凍不住櫃台。
  • Status moves one way: pending → ready → picked up. Enforced in the service, not the UI.狀態單向：待取 → 可取 → 已取。在 service 層強制，不在 UI。
  • Shop name, colour, logo, currency live in tenant_settings. A second shop is one row, no code.店名、主色、logo、幣別在 tenant_settings。第二間店是一列資料，不改程式。
  • Times stored in UTC, shown in Asia/Taipei.時間存 UTC，顯示 Asia/Taipei。

Decisions設計決策

Sizes are constants尺寸是常數

  • Text 18 pt, titles 22 pt, minimum 16. Targets 48 px, main button 64 × 56, cells 80 × 80. All in theme.rs.內文 18 pt、標題 22 pt、最小 16。觸控 48 px、主按鈕 64 × 56、格子 80 × 80。都在 theme.rs。
  • At most three steps for any core action. No dropdowns anywhere.核心操作最多三步。沒有任何下拉選單。

Warn without blocking只警告，不阻擋

  • Stock checks are advisory. The owner has the last word.庫存檢查只是建議。最後決定權在店主。
  • There are no "are you sure" dialogs; undo covers it.沒有「您確定嗎」對話框，靠 Undo 處理。

Save locally first先存本地

  • SQLite with WAL and foreign keys on. Works with no network.SQLite 開 WAL 與 foreign keys。沒網路照常運作。
  • Backup is a snapshot upload. A failure shows one small icon.備份是上傳快照。失敗只亮一個小圖示。

Built from the counter從櫃台長出來的

  • Schema reverse-engineered from photos of the notebook. Flavors in the notebook's order.schema 從訂單簿照片反推。口味照簿子的順序。
  • Feedback came as phone photos of the screen. One of them: "regulars have no phone number, let me skip it."回饋是手機拍的螢幕照片。其中一則：「熟客不會有電話，讓我跳過。」
